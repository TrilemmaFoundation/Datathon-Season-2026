# Build Session 3: Demo Readiness

[Handbook](../README.md) | [Microproduct Guidelines](../guidelines.md) | [Build Session 1](build-session-1.md) | [Build Session 2](build-session-2.md) | [Demo Video Guidelines](../demo-video-guidelines.md)

**The third and final build session is about getting you across the finish line.**

You've framed the problem, utilized data, and started building your microproduct. Now it's time to demonstrate the value you've created.

## What to Expect

**First Hour: Build & Prepare**

Continue building, refining, or polishing whatever you need ahead of Demo Day. Mentors will be circulating to help you get across the finish line.

Prepare a **60-second walkthrough (rough demo)** that convinces us of three things:

1. **The Problem:** What real problem are you solving, and why does it matter?
2. **The Data:** How are you using data to solve that problem?
3. **The Value:** Show us what you've built and the value it creates.

Your product doesn't need to be polished or complete. Focus on communicating its core value proposition.

**Second Hour: Demo Dry Runs & Feedback**

Our mentors will come together as a mock judging panel and circulate between teams.

Each team will have:
- **60 seconds** to demonstrate their microproduct.
- **~2 minutes** to receive feedback from multiple mentors.

Treat this as an opportunity to receive some of the most comprehensive feedback you'll get before Demo Day.  

Different mentors may offer different perspectives. It's up to you to decide which feedback to act on.

## Looking Ahead: Demo Day

After today's session, use the feedback you've received to refine your demo and create your **60-second demo video**, the core deliverable of this Datathon.

Refer to the [Demo Video Guidelines](../demo-video-guidelines.md) for submission requirements and guidance.

Be prepared to present your microproduct live in front of our judges if selected for the Demo Day spotlight (selection will happen live during the event).
