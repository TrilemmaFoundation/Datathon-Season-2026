# 60-Second Demo Video Guidelines

Every team must submit a **60-second demo video** as a core deliverable of Datathon Season 2026.

## Why 60 Seconds?

**Attention is scarce. Value should be clear.**

It's easy to hide behind lengthy explanations. A 60-second constraint forces you to strip away everything that isn't essential and focus on the problem you're solving and the value you've created.

In the real world, you rarely have a captive audience. You need to earn people's attention, not expect it.

**If you only had 60 seconds to convince someone your product matters, what would you show them?**

## What We're Looking For

1. **Sell the problem.** Convince us that you're solving a real problem worth solving. If we don't believe in the problem, nothing else matters.
2. **Show the product.** Walk us through your actual microproduct as though we're the end user. Demonstrate how your product uses data to solve the problem.
3. **Demonstrate value.** Make it obvious what someone gains from using your product. Focus on the outcome, not the technology behind it.

## Tips for Your Demo

- **Show, don't tell.** Prioritize a product walkthrough or screen recording over slides.
- **Use your own voice.** A natural, scripted narration is generally more compelling than generic AI voiceovers or AI-generated videos.
- **Keep technical details to a minimum.** Your audience cares about the value you've created, not your tech stack.
- **Pay attention to the details.** A polished interface and professional presentation can make all the difference in capturing attention. You've focused on solving the problem with data. Now bring out your artistic side and make the value you've created look as compelling as it is. If you're working in a team, this is a great opportunity to lean on someone with an eye for design and presentation.
- **Cut ruthlessly.** Every second should help convince us that your product matters.

## Submission

Submit a **60-second video** demonstrating your microproduct. Use the feedback from [Build Session 3](build%20session%20checklist/build-session-3.md) to refine your story before recording.

Your video should stand on its own, without requiring additional explanation.

Submit your video and GitHub repository through the [final submission form](https://airtable.com/appZZI7zTz3xYJE0r/pagSrc0WDreG1SxeI/form).

**Remember: You're not pitching the technology you've built. You're demonstrating the value you've created.**
